## 2. Ordering rule (coverage, not yield)

`plan/REVIEW-2026-10-18.md` finding 4b: the caption queue was ordered by
expected yield, which biases which bodies get transcribed. This run states
and applies a **coverage rule** instead:

> Bodies are processed in ascending order of their count of **public**
> density turns already in the corpus (the NIMBY-pilot frame:
> `output/nimby_pilot/turns_scored.csv`, density|both episodes, turns ≥ 25
> words, role public). A body absent from the frame counts as 0. Within a
> body, meetings go most-recent first (the cutter's own order). Expected
> yield, clip length and tag are never consulted.
