# plan/hostility.txt -- hostility word list for the A1 lexicon pass
# (plan/ANALYSIS-transit.md 3, first bullet: "a hostility word-list rate").
# Seeded 2026-09-23 for the T1 pilot. Read by analysis/a1_sentiment.py.
#
# WHAT THIS IS AND IS NOT. VADER scores valence: "terrible traffic" and "I am
# terrified for my children" both come out strongly negative, and neither is
# hostile. This list is the second channel -- speech aimed AT someone: contempt,
# accusation, insult, threat. It is deliberately narrow. A negative VADER score
# with a zero hostility rate is the ordinary case (an unhappy resident); the two
# together are the case worth a human read.
#
# Format (same reader as plan/frames.yaml, analysis/a0_terms.load_frames): a
# category is a top-level `key:` line followed by `- term` lines; '#' comments;
# terms match whole-word, case-insensitive, optional 's, and a space in a term
# matches whitespace or a hyphen.
#
# LIMITS, stated so the rate is not over-read:
#   * Single words cannot see sarcasm, tone of voice, or a civil sentence with
#     a hostile point ("I would ask the board to explain its priorities").
#   * Civic meetings are procedurally polite. Expect a low rate and a long tail
#     of zeros; report it as a floor on hostility, never as its level.
#   * Terms that did not occur in the pilot stay in the list, marked "0 hits",
#     so the next corpus reports them rather than losing them silently.
#   * This is a SEED list, not a validated instrument. The paid Claude pass
#     (0-4 hostility, meanness-scale anchored) is what would calibrate it.
#
# READ 2026-09-23, T1 pilot. The first draft fired 13 times and 11 of the 13
# were wrong (analysis/a1_sentiment.py KWIC). The terms that produced them are
# now in `ambiguous_*` categories: they are still counted and still reported,
# but they do NOT enter the headline hostility rate. The evidence, verbatim:
#   entitled   3 dais hits, ALL land-use ("the units that have already been
#              entitled", "a report entitled ..."). This is the technical
#              vocabulary of the corpus, not contempt.
#   lie        1 dais hit, the verb ("where the tenant protection should lie").
#   accountable 2 public hits, both the ordinary civic register and one of them
#              quoting a grand jury ("failed to hold VTA staff accountable").
#   lawsuit    2 public hits, both DESCRIBING third-party litigation
#              ("they have eight active lawsuits"), neither a threat.
#   don't care 1 public hit, not aimed at anyone ("I don't care whether I'm
#              travelling in Europe").
# After the split, 2 of 173 public turns and 0 of 621 dais turns are hostile.

# AMBIGUOUS. Counted and reported, but NOT in the hostility rate: every one of
# these fired on something that was not hostility in the pilot (see the READ
# note at the top). They stay because the same word IS hostile in other mouths
# -- "you lied to us" is not "where the line should lie" -- and a list that
# silently dropped them would hide the failure mode from the next corpus.
# analysis/a1_sentiment.py keys on the `ambiguous_` prefix.

ambiguous_legal:
  # Descriptive in a civic meeting far more often than threatening.
  - sue
  - lawsuit
  - litigation
