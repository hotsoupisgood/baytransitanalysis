**This is the one fit where role × renter share clears 95 %:** −0.363 (SE 0.120,

turn-level reading of an instrument with no κ gate, and it was not a
pre-registered hypothesis — so it belongs in the write-up as something to look
at next, not as a finding.

| within-side rows / role | 1,892 / +0.429 (+0.161, +0.700) | **2,337 / +0.592 (+0.349, +0.841)** |
| any-side rows / role | 3,950 / +2.422 (+2.183, +2.669) | **5,106 / +2.340 (+2.137, +2.553)** |
| hostility rows / role | not fitted | **9,777 / +2.466 (+2.240, +2.698)** |

The headline that moved is the within-side role gap: **+0.429 → +0.592**, and
the interval narrowed and moved clear of the 10-19 lower bound. 445 more
stance-bearing turns, 173 more episodes and 41 more meetings entered the fit.
The any-side gap went the other way by a smaller amount, +2.422 → +2.340, with
a tighter interval; its body variance is still non-zero, so it is still the one
fit of the three that profiles at three levels. Hostility is new today.

The tail shares barely moved — opposed and hostile are flat, supportive fell
0.015 — which is what you want from a 21 % larger frame: the role contrast is
not an artefact of which meetings happened to be transcribed. The public split
drifted 1 point toward neutral.

Caveat on the intervals: lme4's profiler needed `devtol = 0.05` for the any-side
and within-side fits today, against 0.01 on 10-19 (bobyqa stops ~0.02 deviance
short of the optimum the profiler then finds, and restarting from the fitted
values does not close it; the hostility fits still profiled at 0.01). That is a
tolerance problem, not a validity problem, but those two role CIs are a shade
wider than they would otherwise be.
