# Independent methodological review — ytpt transit/density study (2026-10-18)

Reviewer stance: sceptical, MSc-level Bayesian text study. Read-only; nothing run, spent or edited. Evidence cited as file/section. Severity: C critical (paper rejected), H high, M medium, L low.

## Ranked findings

**1 (C). There is no human-coded gold set anywhere in the chain.** `output/t0/label_sheet.csv` has 0 of 100 labels filled (checked today); the 12-clip listening set (`output/validation/listen/README.md`) has no corrections; the stance κ gate (SHOWCASE 4a) is unpassed. Every recall, precision, WER and stance number is Sonnet versus Sonnet or WhisperX versus YouTube ASR. DECISIONS #14's reopen clause cites a "hand-labelled set" that does not exist. *Remedy:* a gold set. Draw from the 8 whole-meeting GPU transcripts already on disk plus 4 more covering agenda_index, Swagit and a second board (≈$0.5 GPU). Unit: 60-s window. Sample every window any arm flagged plus a random 20 % of unflagged windows (inverse-probability weights recover recall); ≈500 windows per coder. Two coders (Puck, Wren) blind to arm labels; 5-category Krippendorff's α ≥ 0.7, about-vs-not κ ≥ 0.8; disagreements resolved by discussion with the rule written down. *Cost:* ≈12 h per coder, ≈$1.

**2 (C). The v2 validation is circular and one-sided.** Reference = Sonnet v1 on 60-s windows (RECALL-CHECK "Method" 3). Adjudication reviewed only the 11 windows v2 over-called, all flipped to v2's label, by reading v2's rationale and an excerpt, not audio (PILOT §8, §9 caveats; `codebook_v2_adjudicate.py` flips exactly those minutes). v2's misses, v1-only calls and agreements were never reviewed. A reference corrected only where the instrument under test disagreed cannot measure that instrument; 0.811/0.873 is a ceiling, not an estimate. Four clusters, two bodies, one route, 37 transit minutes in two meetings make the intervals decorative. *Remedy:* symmetric blind adjudication (equal draws of v2 over-calls, v2 misses, v1-only calls, agreements, arm hidden), then finding 1. *Cost:* ≈2 h for 60 windows.

**3 (H). The construct moved mid-study, in the wrong order.** "Transit" is v1 (public transportation + street reallocation, `classify.py` INSTRUCTIONS), then v2 (+ active transportation, service planning), then v2.1 "anything not car related" (PILOT §8a, one WhatsApp sentence, not in code: 0 hits for "car related" in `classify.py`, unmeasured). Sequence actually followed: edit codebook → pilot → adjudicate the pilot's own over-calls → write the definition → plan re-read. Required order: definition → codebook → gold set → validation → read. §8a's rule that future disagreements are adjudicated by "a second pinned LLM pass" is not acceptable: the adjudicator must be independent of the instrument. "Report v1 and v2 as separate facets, never pooled" is honest but not a remedy: a body read under v1 for part of the window and v2 for the rest has no comparable rate across time, and the 152 Spanish windows stay v1 forever (PROPOSED-14 §2). *Remedy:* freeze v2.1 text and hash now; validate on the gold set; read the whole corpus under one instrument; v1 becomes a sensitivity appendix. *Cost:* $18–30 Sonnet once, after finding 1.

**4 (H). Estimand and measurand diverge at seven points.**
(a) Target: minutes of meeting time on the topic. Measured: located minutes after a screen with recall 0.30–0.86 by family/route and precision 0.45–0.84 (RECALL-CHECK tables). Rate = true rate × recall(body, route, family) + false positives.
(b) Population: all regular Bay Area meetings. Measured: 29 of 39 bodies; 4 of 7 transit boards unlocated (T2-PLAN §6); 255 of 614 meetings uncaptioned (WAVE1 §2). The model's exposure is only `cut`/`no_episodes` meetings (`load.py` docstring), and the caption queue is **ordered by measured yield** (T2-PLAN §3h). Exposure is therefore selected on the outcome: high-yield bodies get located first, so the "complete coverage" set and the partial bodies' floors are both yield-biased, and month effects are confounded with locate timing.
(c) Roles: staff folded into dais (STANCE-S1 §2), invited presenters labelled dais, 5–6 % unknown; 93.6 % accuracy is post-tuning on the same 10 clips (T0 test 6; the untuned set it recommends was never drawn).
(d) Stance frame: transcribed turns only, after a per-body cap and whichever GPU batch ran; BART 25 % and VTA 11 % transcribed (WAVE1 §3).
(e) `about_both` minutes are credited to both families (models README §1).
(f) At operator boards nearly every item is "about transit" (RECALL-CHECK BART caveat); board and council transit rates are different constructs.
(g) 60-s reference windows under a "most of the window" rule versus 240/120-s pipeline windows: the recall metric itself mixes two granularity constructs (PILOT §2 correction).
*Remedy:* a one-page estimand statement (target population, unit, measurand, nuisance terms) and a selection diagram; stop yield-ordering or model it; coverage-restricted estimates as primary. *Cost:* 1 day, $0.

**5 (H). The screened design biases cross-body comparison.** Misses are vocabulary: active transportation (Richmond), operations/maintenance (BART), affordable-housing allocation (Los Altos, Milpitas), plus caption homophones (fare→fair, YIMBY dropped). Vocabulary is body-specific, so recall is body-specific, and a route-level stratum `gamma_r` absorbs none of that. Full-ASR locate raises recall to 0.90–0.93 (FULLASR) but moves the error to the classifier's definition and precision (transit precision 0.36–0.53), which inflates rather than deflates rates and needs the same gold set. *Remedy:* per-body recall sampling: 2 meetings per body, exhaustive Sonnet labels (≈230 h × $0.083 ≈ $19 Sonnet, ≈$2.5 GPU) scored against the gold-calibrated reference; then decide screen vs full ASR with numbers. *Cost:* ≈$22, 1 day.

**6 (M-H). Routes-as-strata is not a measurement-error model.** The stratum assumes a constant multiplier per route and recall 1 on the reference route. Sketch: observed minutes y ~ NB(E·λ_bmf·ρ_brf + E·κ_rf, φ), with ρ_rf ~ Beta from the validation binomial (found | about minutes), body-level jitter log ρ_brf = log ρ_rf + ε_b, ε_b ~ N(0, σ_ρ) with σ_ρ from the per-meeting recall spread (0.32–0.92), and a false-positive rate κ_rf from precision. Fit validation and corpus jointly so ρ and λ are estimated together and λ's interval widens honestly. *Cost:* 1–2 days, $0; it needs finding 1 for the Beta inputs.

**7 (M). Post-expansion recall is tuned on the test set.** The 57 terms and the homophone alias layer were derived from the missed-window lists of the same meetings on which recall was then re-scored (RECALL-CHECK "Keyword screen expansion"; `plan/keywords_aliases_auto.txt`). Transit screen recall 0.52→0.83 is in-sample. The YouTube arm's "byte-identical" result is a non-test (no new term occurs there), not out-of-sample confirmation. *Remedy:* freeze the keyword hash before scoring; score only on held-out meetings (the second BART and two Palo Alto meetings already on disk, ≈$1.1); report per-term hit counts. *Cost:* ≈$1, 2 h.

**8 (M). The stance pass ran before its gate.** SHOWCASE 4a makes κ ≥ 0.6 on 150 blind-coded turns a precondition for the full pass; S1 (6,100 turns, ≈$45) is in progress today (`output/stance_s1/collect.log` 16:46 UTC). 97.8 % k-sample self-consistency is reliability, not validity; 41 % not_applicable; staff reports (codebook stance 0) sit inside "dais". *Remedy:* double-code 150 turns now, weighted κ, before any S1 number is quoted. *Cost:* ≈5 h, $0.

**9 (M). Spend order is instrument polish before validation.** Planned: v2.1 re-read $18, stance $45–60 (spent), full ASR $105–130 deferred. The $0.25 v2.1 check (PILOT §9 caveat) has not run. Correct order: gold set ($1, ~25 h human) → v2.1 check ($0.25) → held-out recall ($1) → per-body recall ($22) → one re-read under the final instrument ($18–30) → stance. Full ASR is the one spend that fixes the screen; defer it until precision is known, not indefinitely.

**10 (L–M). Other reviewer objections.**
- Two Richmond meetings with 5 % ASR coverage sit in the exposure with zero located minutes: instrument failure counted as a true zero. Exclude locate tracks under ~60 words/h from exposure (`caption_audit.py` already has the rule).
- Boards: n = 3 located. Any board-versus-council claim is three bodies.
- The last month of the window is partial (discovery 2026-09-22).
- Which episodes get transcribed under the per-body cap must be random, not queue order, or the stance frame is selected.
- 4-cluster percentile bootstraps are not confidence intervals; report ranges.
- Ethics: no data-protection basis or ethics sign-off is recorded; redaction PERSON recall is unmeasured (WAVE1 §7); the 30-turn check is pending.
- Reproducibility: DECISIONS #12–#19 still read "pending Puck"; 44 uncommitted files; validation artefacts uncommitted.

## Three things the project does well

1. **Instrument hygiene.** Every row carries codebook hash, model, keyword-list version, max-run and route; `facets.py` refuses to pool; route is recorded as the route, not the model. Few MSc projects get this right.
2. **Measurement honesty.** Recall was measured at all, misses are listed by cause with excerpts, the padded-bounds artefact was found and corrected, estimates are logged against actual bills, and the write-ups flag their own weak clusters.
3. **Cheap, reusable validation and a sound model skeleton.** Exhaustive references cost $1–2 and recompute for $0; the NB model with exposure offset, body hierarchy, zero-inflation and posterior predictive checks is the right shape and only needs the error terms above.
