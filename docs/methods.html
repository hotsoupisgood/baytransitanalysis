EXPLORATORY. Loading the stamp…

This study asks how people at Bay Area public meetings feel about housing density and transit. Every score on this page is on the proposal that was being discussed, not on housing or transit in general.

Because every score is on the proposal under discussion, a separate pass corrects the direction before the YIMBY and NIMBY labels are formed. For each discussion stretch it takes the agenda item title and the first 600 words of the stretch, officials and staff first, and records whether the proposal would add or restrict housing, whether it would add or restrict transit, or whether the proposal is not about how much gets built or how much service runs. Each stretch is coded three times and the majority label is kept. Where the proposal restricts housing or transit, the sign of every score in that stretch is flipped, so the corrected score measures support for more housing or transit rather than support for the proposal. Stretches left out: rent rules and other proposals about housing but not about building it, open public comment and agendas with several items at once, stretches where the three codings differed, and statements whose own topic the proposal does not touch. Of 1,212 scored stretches, 729 are a single proposal, 408 survive every exclusion and 54 of those have the sign flipped; the three codings differed on 102. The instrument is dir-fe5e7ffebe/claude-sonnet-4-6 through the Message Batches API, and it cost $6.26 on 2026-10-24.

On a proposal that restricts housing, such as rent rules, support for the proposal is not support for more housing, which is why those proposals are left out of the labels rather than counted with the sign they carry.

The four LLM instruments this study runs on, quoted in full, with their hashes, the model behind each one, what was measured about them, and what is wrong with those measurements. Every block on this page is read out of the repo at build time by export/export_methods.py, not typed here. A test recomputes each block's hash from the source, so the page cannot drift from the instrument.

Transcript quotations on this site are transcribed speech, with names, home addresses and contact details removed by pipeline/redact.py, and cuts inside a quotation marked “…”.
