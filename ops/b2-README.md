# Bay Area transit & housing meetings

Recordings and transcripts of Bay Area city council and transit board meetings
from September 2025 to September 2026, collected for a study of how councils
and the public talk about housing and transit.
Project: https://github.com/hotsoupisgood/baytransitanalysis

**To listen to a meeting:** open `meetings`, pick the council or agency, then
the date. Each file is one whole meeting, named by date and title.
`meetings/INDEX.csv` is a spreadsheet of every meeting with a link to the
original video. Files are .opus or .m4a; VLC or a web browser plays both.

**Everything else** is the project's working files:

- `output`: audio as downloaded, and the transcripts (`output/redacted` has
  names and addresses removed)
- `plan`: the list of meetings in the study
- `state`: database backups

New meetings are added every night. Nothing here is ever deleted.
